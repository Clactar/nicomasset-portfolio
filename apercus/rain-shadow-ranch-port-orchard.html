What’s growing in the field

Over 50 types of spring to fall annuals are grown at Rain Shadow Ranch. We focus on varieties suited to the temperate PNW climate, but also dabble in a few Mediterranean and hot climate flowers.

Spring

Ranunculus, anemone, snapdragons, stock, narcissus and tulips

Summer

Dahlias, sunflowers, zinnias, hydrangea, statice, strawflower, ageratum, cosmos and yarrow

Fall

Wheat, autumn foliage, corn, tomatoes, peppers, marigold and most of the summer varieties

Herbs

Sage, rosemary, Pacific bay, basil, parsley, dill and cilantro

We are not organic certified, but do practice environmentally friendly, sustainable farming, including Integrated Pest Management, organic liquid seaweed and kelp fertilizer, and compost teas.

Our blossoms are harvested at their peak, ensuring vibrant colors and long-lasting beauty.

For florists and designers

We grow specialty cut flowers for local florists, designers and wholesale customers throughout the South Sound and Kitsap Peninsula.

We hand-harvest during the cooler morning hours, then place every bloom in clean, cool water and temperature-controlled storage before distribution.

Event and wholesale pick-up and delivery are by appointment only.

Is the flower field open to the public?

Our field is not open to the public, except for scheduled Pick-Your-Own hours or reservation-only events and workshops.

Can I rent the field for a wedding?

Our field may be rented for outdoor elopements or intimate weddings of 25 people or less, with affordable floral packages for pick-up at the Ranch. Event Florals

Do you ship flowers?

Deliveries within 15 miles of Port Orchard, WA. Nationwide shipping for wreaths and all non-perishable goods.
