open air beginner's wheelthrowing

2 hours

weekends at 2 or 4:30 pm, or by private appointment

try out the potter's wheel in our outdoor garden studio and turn soft clay into objects you can use every day. you'll (more than likely) make bowls or cups, add decoration with stamps and texture tools if you'd like, and pick your favorite one to keep at the end.

yes, it is a bit of a jungle

the garden and grounds are part of our certified wildlife habitat: food, water, shelter and places to raise young. you'll walk along winding paths we made from our old fence, among butterflies, bees, lizards, frogs, birds, one cat, one dog and a couple humans.

meet the lizards

it's not just about the clay. this medium proves over and over again to be a conduit for community, good people and sweet times.

liza is a born n' raised hollywood floridian. she has been making pots since 2014, after finding wheelthrowing while writing sociology papers at the university of north florida, and started teaching the wheel about six years ago, before she had her own space.

dirty lizard studio is a place and ongoing project started by two south floridian ceramicists, eliza furr and nick abrami, in the summer of 2023: a quiet place to rest, to learn without limitation and to relentlessly create, a third space between consumption and production.
