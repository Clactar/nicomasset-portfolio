It starts with a feeling, an idea, a moment that calls out to be saved, to be immortalized in the world of light and color.

For BillieJo, custom work is all about listening. A side window, a memorial piece, a memory from the trip of a lifetime: she turns something you can't hold into a work of art that fits your heart and your home.

  1. Conversation

    It begins with listening to your idea.

  2. The cartoon

    A full-size pattern, drawn from that idea.

  3. Choosing glass

    The hardest part, and the one that sets the light.

  4. Cut and fit

    Each piece cut and assembled like a puzzle.

  5. Grind, foil, solder

    Edges ground, foiled, then soldered together.

  6. Wash and polish

    Every detail finished before it goes home.

Learn glass at the studio.

Hands-on classes for all ages and abilities, from mosaics to fused glass to stained glass, with all the materials at hand.

  • Beginner stained glass course

    $150plus supplies

    Five classes, starting from your own idea: pattern design, choosing and cutting glass, grinding, foiling, soldering, patina, polish and framing.

    $20per student, per class

    Wednesdays, 9 to 11 am. A short art history lesson, a technique demo, then a hands-on project. Supplies included. Children under 5 come with a parent; bring a snack and water.

Hi, I'm BillieJo.

I'm the artist behind Angel Dreams Studios. I'm always working on something, a custom piece or a personal project, and I love sharing art through teaching and making it together. Let's create something beautiful!

  • Raised in Central Wisconsin, by a family of makers: ceramics, quilting, and the kitchen.
  • Over 20 years in New England, where she studied fine arts and found stained glass.
  • Apprenticed at The Glass Source in Seymour, Connecticut.
  • Restoration work on the windows of Plumb Memorial Library, Shelton, and Beth Israel Synagogue, New Haven.
  • Exhibited at the Yale School of Art with "Looking In: Organs and How We View Them".
  • Board member of River Valley Arts.
