Scrap metal, new life.

Cast-off, discarded and scrapped metals, welded by Tim Pace into sculpture for your garden, your mantel or your desk. Every piece is made one at a time, then signed and numbered.

If it can be welded, it can be art.

Working in the medium of found art metal sculpture, I create unique pieces by welding together recycled metals. With such an array of resources, there is no limit to what can be made, and many times the materials themselves decide what the outcome will be.

An electrician who picked up a welder

“Found art is created by taking an object that already exists and turning it into art.”

I have no formal art training. I was an electrician by trade, until a gift, a pile of scrap and a request changed things. Today my pieces range from works that fit on a tabletop to eight-foot-tall sculptures: people, animals, flowers, insects and abstracts, to name a few.

  • 1995The gift of a welder, an excess of scrap metal and a request for a unique gift. The result: a three-foot-tall golfer for a garden. Dogs and frogs followed.
  • 1998Eco-Art becomes a full-time pursuit.
  • 2007Tim and his work featured on episode 2112 of Nashville Public Television’s Tennessee Crossroads.

Member of the Tennessee Association of Craft Artists, the Craftsmen’s Guild of Mississippi and the Tennessee Valley Art Association.
