Longarm quilting

Complete our reservation form to have your quilt professionally quilted. From baby to king size, you choose the thread color and the design, or let D'Sewist choose them and supply the batting.

  1. Fill in the reservation formName, size, thread color and type of design.
  2. We call youWhen we are ready to have your quilt sent to us.
  3. Send us your quiltIf you mail it, we'll call so you know your baby has arrived safely.
  4. We ship it back for freeOnce quilted, it comes home to you at no charge.
