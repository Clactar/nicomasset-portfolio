We crossed paths at Mudflat Pottery School in Somerville, Massachusetts, hitched up in 1999, and moved to Maine. We renovated the cow shed into a studio and set up shop as Cedar Mountain Potters. We've been making a living in clay ever since.

David Orser

David holds an MFA from Cranbrook Academy of Art. He began working in clay in 1977, when he attended the Museum of Fine Arts school in Boston. Vases, tea bowls, bowls and platters, tiles, jars and sculpture.

Laurel MacDuffie

Laurel holds an MA in Art Therapy from Lesley University in Cambridge, Massachusetts. She's been making art all her life, and working specifically in clay since 1997.
