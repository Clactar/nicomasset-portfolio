Art for everyone, anywhere

That's our motto. A rotating exhibition hangs throughout the house, chosen to feed your spirit and inspire your own creativity, and all exhibited art is available for purchase.

We are committed to supporting Wisconsin's artists, and we proudly take part in Bay View Gallery Night, the neighborhood's free, self-guided art crawl. On the first floor, the gift shop sells souvenirs made by local Milwaukee artists.

On our walls over the years: Joel Richter, Stacy Ollmann, Thom Ertl, Sharon Mergener, John Kowalczyk, September Rain, Jamie Allard, Christina Zawadiwsky, Natalie Shumylo, Elias Zananiri and Cheryl Ray. In 2021, John Kowalczyk led a community project here: 100 neighbors, friends and family painted a mural together on a wall of the property.

“It would not be ‘Your Mama’s B&B!’ No doilies or great grandma’s wedding dress in the hallway would be allowed!”

Mary Ellen Hermann, who runs The Muse with Andrew Meechan

Breakfast at 8:30, with Mary Ellen and Andrew

They were pen pals from the age of 11 and 12½, a girl on the south side of Milwaukee and an English schoolboy. In their 50s they chose the United States, and Bay View, and turned this house into a B&B: high ceilings, beautiful woodwork, bay windows and an antique fireplace, with modern and artful touches.

Breakfast is the highlight of their day. Mary Ellen usually cooks, and if you are lucky, Andrew might whip up a full English breakfast to show you how it's done.
