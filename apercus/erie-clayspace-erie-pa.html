One night, or eight weeks.

From one-day introductory workshops to multi-week classes, on the wheel or by hand. Dates are on our class schedule.

  • Mud to Mugs

    A fun, beginner-friendly night out: turn a slab of clay into your very own handmade mug. No experience needed.

  • MYO Tiki Tumblers

    Craft one-of-a-kind tiki cups and bring some tropical vibes to your next cocktail.

  • Sip & Spin

    Bring a drink, bring a friend: our laid-back, BYOB class where you learn the basics of throwing on the wheel.

  • Tandem Throw: Pottery Date Night

    Grab a partner, friend, spouse, coworker or actual date. Learn the basics solo, then team up for a tandem session: trust, creativity and a little clay-covered chaos.

  • Try It! One Day of Wheel Throwing

    Curious about the pottery wheel? The perfect beginner-friendly way to dip your hands into clay.

Host a private party.

Two hours of personalized instruction from a professional teaching artist. Bring your group to throw bowls on the wheel, handbuild a custom mug, or sculpt your pet.

Included: a dedicated ECS instructor; clay, tools, glazes and all firing, bisque and glaze; studio setup and cleanup; tables for food and drinks (BYOB for adults 21+). Finished pieces are ready in 2 to 4 weeks.
