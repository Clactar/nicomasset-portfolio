Recycled rag rugs

Chris Gustin is a fiber artist who takes recycling seriously. Most of the material in our rugs is reclaimed: new fabric that is the byproduct of another industry, from hosiery mills, bedding factories, curtain manufacturers, and clothing and upholstery mills.

To those industries, the material is waste. To us, it is the heart of a recycled rug. We turn over a ton of it into art every year.

Our handwoven rugs are certified as Indiana Artisan quality work. Every rug is one of a kind, and the selection changes often.

Come weave with us

Classes and day instruction are given on the rug looms. They run throughout the year as weather permits, so ask if you're interested.

A day-weaving rug class

  1. We pick the colors of fabric for the rug you want to make.
  2. I show you how to plan a rug, and we pull the pieces and lay out the design.
  3. We put the material on shuttles, with a short how-to on weaving it.
  4. Once you get the hang of it, you weave. I check on you every 10 minutes or so and advance the warp when you need it.
  5. I put in the header thread, cut the rug off the loom and tie the knots for you.
