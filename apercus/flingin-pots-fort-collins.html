Like playing with fire?

Check out our Monthly Raku Workshops!!

Inspired by traditional Japanese raku firing, our Western-style raku pulls pottery from the kiln at bright red heat and places it into containers with combustible materials. Once they ignite, the containers are closed, and the reduction brings out brilliant metallic blues, purples, coppers, reds and yellows.

  • Each person paints and fires two pieces in a 2 hour workshop
  • Your pieces go home with you after the event: the only ceramic process where you paint, fire and take home!
  • Private raku workshop for your group: $55 / person, min 6, max 14
