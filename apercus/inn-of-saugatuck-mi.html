A Sears and Roebuck catalogue house, built in 1904.

The inn was originally built in 1904 as the residence for the Koenig Hardware Store owners. It was the second largest version of a Sears and Roebuck catalogue house, at an original cost of $1800.

For nearly two decades it was an art gallery, the Highbanks Gallery of Saugatuck. It then became the Saugatuck Victorian Inn, and in 2017 it took its name: The Inn of Saugatuck.

The special craftsmanship of the restoration is evident everywhere you turn. Our historic inn keeps all of the original oak woodwork and unique Victorian trim.

Take an early morning stroll before breakfast, or sip your coffee on the front porch in the cool lake breeze.

Things to do in Saugatuck

Saugatuck is a town surrounded by nature, with something for everyone. Looking to relax? Try the beaches and art galleries. Want to work up a sweat? There are hikes, bikes, and watersports. Looking for more adventure? How about a dune ride or charter fishing.
