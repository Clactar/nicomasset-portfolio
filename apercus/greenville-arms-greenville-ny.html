A Victorian inn with a gallery, a chocolate kitchen and an espresso bar.

Built in 1889 for William S. and Mary Reed Chapman Vanderbilt, the Inn sits on six acres of gardens, lawns and woodlands in Greenville. Sixteen guest rooms, open year-round, so you can come back for every season.

Since 1982, the Inn has welcomed artists and instructors from around the world to learn, create and relax.

Hudson River Valley Art Workshops run from March to December, painting and fiber art alike. The Inn also runs the only independently owned art supply shop in Greene County carries paints, canvas, fabric and notions.

A bit of history

  1. 1889

    William S. Vanderbilt builds a farmhouse in the country town of Greenville. A generation later come the turret, the grand dining rooms and the Victorian details.

  2. 1952

    The Stevens family turns the property into a boarding house for summer guests while raising their four daughters.

  3. 1980

    Two of the daughters, Laura and Barbara, become the innkeepers and found the Hudson River Valley Art Workshops with local painters.

  4. 2004

    The LaPollas take ownership and add a fiber art program, stretching the workshops from March to December.
