Our bed chambers

Hardwood floors, Persian rugs, poster beds and soft matelassé bedding. Each bed chamber is decorated with beautiful 18th and 19th century New England antiques and furnishings.

  • Bed Chamber One

    Queen poster bed

    A large bed chamber with a separate sitting room and a fireplace beside the bed. Keurig coffee maker, tea, a wine chiller and glasses in the sitting room desk. New bathroom with Kohler fixtures and a 4′ step-in shower.

  • Bed Chamber Two

    Queen poster bed

    A large bed chamber with a large separate sitting room, an electric fireplace, comfy furniture and a “cricket” table. Hand-stenciled floors, and a newly remodeled bathroom with Kohler fixtures and a 4′ step-in shower.

  • Bed Chamber Three

    Double poster bed

    A cozy chamber under a crocheted bed canopy, with linen covers, a Persian rug, comfy furniture by a faux fireplace and a full bath with a 3′ step-in shower.

  • Olde Homestead Cottage

    Pet friendly

    Originally the “summer kitchen” of the stone farmhouse, this free-standing 400 sq ft cottage sits 10 ft from the Inn, with its own full bathroom and a fenced patio.

Three common areas, all yours

“Of course everything is hidden to keep the colonial feeling alive. Remember, you’ve just been transported to the 1800s.”

The full kitchen is snuggled behind the cage bar in the tavern room. The induction stove top sits on an old cupboard, and a Breville oven, a microwave and a coffee pot are hiding in the cabinet next to it. All common areas are included in your bed chamber price.

Gathering roomHistoric books, and games in the New England corner cabinet behind the game table.
