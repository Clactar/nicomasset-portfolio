A small piece of land, a few beef cattle and ‘Pinky’ the boar.

That is how we started in 2010. Since then we have expanded every year to what is now 4 different farm locations, with fully sustainable livestock including beef cattle, pigs, chickens and lamb, and most recently a huge variety of fresh vegetables.

As kids, we grew up on our own family farms using regenerative agriculture, so when it came time to start our own farm, this practice was just second nature. We proudly follow strict animal welfare protocol and provide all natural, non-GMO meats and veggies.

What is regenerative agriculture?

Farming and grazing practices that rebuild the soil’s organic matter and restore degraded soil biodiversity. The result is carbon draw-down, improvements on the water cycle and healthier soil.

Flavorful, nutritious foods, grown sustainably.

Our animals are raised with care and love in the rolling hillsides of Washington County, because raising animals humanely is an important part of our farm.

  • Grass-fed beef

    100% grass-fed and grass-finished, on pasture all summer and our own harvested hay for the winter.

  • Heritage breed pork

    Raised on pasture with non-GMO, local grains. All our smoked products have no nitrites.

  • Pasture-raised chicken

    Heritage breeds like Freedom Rangers and Kosher Kings, vigorous foragers on free-range pastures.

  • Grass-fed lamb

    Free-range, grass-fed and grass-finished. A lean, flavorful meat, from chops to shanks.

  • Fresh produce

    Organic seeds, limited soil amendments, and we rarely spray for pests.

  • Organic seasoning mixes

    Homemade, with carefully selected herbs and spices, free from artificial additives.

Raised with special care from day one.

Each animal is grass-fed and grass-finished. Grass-finished beef is about 20% lower in calories than grain-finished beef and has higher levels of Omega-3 fatty acids, and Vitamins A and E.

All of our beef is USDA-approved, all-natural, and non-GMO with no regular use of antibiotics.
