Portraits and commissions

My colorful style is perfectly suited for commissioned portraits of a client or their beloved family, pets, objects, landscape or home. Families, couples, grandchildren and pets have all been great fun to create.

  1. Our conversationThe process begins with a talk, by email, phone or in person.
  2. A paper sketchI develop it from your ideas, and ask questions to help you visualize if you are not sure.
  3. The canvasA sketch on the canvas, from which I create the painting.
  4. Your approvalAfter your final approval I have it professionally photographed.
  5. Ready to hangAfter about a week of drying time it is ready for your home.

The process takes about three to four weeks. A payment plan is easy to set up. This will be fun!

About Stacie

A little background

With my painting I aspire to beauty, connection, celebration, and love, and I hope, something lasting.

Originally from Long Island, I studied art at the State University of New York at New Paltz and fell in love with the Mid-Hudson Valley. I have a husband and two grown sons, and a variety of cats and dogs have been family. They turn up as figures in some of my paintings!

My art embraces Expressionism’s sense of freedom, the brilliant color of the Fauves, and Pop’s animation. Favorite painters include Van Gogh, Bonnard, Matisse, Franz Marc, David Hockney and Keith Haring.
