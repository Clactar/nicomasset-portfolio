The personal studio of Katie Bartle.

Her love for the outdoors is a theme that keeps appearing in her work.

Katie is a ceramic and mixed media artist from Port Huron, Michigan. She has been working with clay for over 20 years and painting since the age of 2. In 2018 she decided it was time to share her love for art and craft by offering classes to the community, and KB Studios was born. She started teaching pottery at Studio 1219, a community studio, and now has her own space to offer even more opportunities to be creative.

At a young age she received accolades from the Ward Museum of Wildfowl Art in Salisbury, Maryland, for waterfowl decoy carving, and she still makes folk art decoys and lures for friends and family.
