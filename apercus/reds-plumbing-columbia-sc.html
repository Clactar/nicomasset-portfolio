What Reds takes care of

“Service plumbing, drain lines, water lines, water heaters, and tankless.”

That is how Reds sums it up. Here is what it looks like on real jobs, in their own words from the last few months.

  • Drains

    Sink drains and drain lines that keep backing up.

  • Sewer lines

    Massive roots pulled back, and the line replaced from the house to the city tap.

  • Water lines and repipes

    Old galvanized pipe swapped out so the water pressure comes back.

  • Water heaters and tankless

    Water heater replacement and tank to tankless conversions.

    A 200 ft well pulled and replaced, pressure tanks, pressure regulating valves.

  • Small repairs

    Shutoff valves, outdoor spigots and water leaks around the house.
