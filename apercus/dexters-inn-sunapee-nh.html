Three ways to stay at Dexter's

All of our guest rooms are warm, welcoming and comfortable. Each has a private bathroom, and each is unique in size and decoration. Every room comes with two signature Dexter's Inn robes and our signature bath and body collection.

The LaPorte Family

Years ago, families came to the grand hotels of New Hampshire's Sunapee area to find their summer respite among the mountains and lakes. Dexter's Inn continues this proud tradition of innkeeping in the same friendly and unassuming manner as has been its practice for more than 60 years.

Maria LaPorte's first time at Dexter's Inn, she was a guest at 3 months old. She spent her winters skiing Mt. Sunapee and her summers swimming in the lake, and today she lives on site with her two young children, P.J. and Georgia.

Having grown up in Sunapee, she can guide guests to the best food, must-see places and fun activities she has personally experienced.

Your very own country estate wedding

The wedding couple reserves the entire property for the weekend: exclusive use of the buildings, grounds and activities, and the innkeepers' undivided attention.

With room for 50+ overnight guests, your celebration can extend beyond the ceremony and reception to an entire weekend with family and friends.

We also host family reunions, business meetings, craft workshops, church retreats and private parties such as bridal showers, baby showers and holiday parties. Write to info@dextersnh.com with your wedding questions or to request cost and availability.
