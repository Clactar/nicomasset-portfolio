Magnolia Place is here for making memories

Step through our front door and settle into your room or suite. The history of the old farmhouse is part of every corner of the inn.

Enjoy the wraparound porches, the lake views and the relaxing common areas. Have a hot beverage in the Garden Room, play some music on our family’s baby grand piano, then hike the property and watch the sunset from the Adirondack chairs by the fire pit.

Nestled among the region’s finest wineries, we are just minutes from Watkins Glen International Speedway, with Cornell University and Ithaca College a short drive away.

Our rooms and suites

Original artisan creations, locally made lavender soaps and beds draped in the finest linens. Many rooms open onto our two-story wraparound porch, and each private suite has a jetted spa tub, soaking tub or hot tub.

The suites

Fireplaces, spa tubs and private porch access.

  • Magnolia SuiteLofty ceilings, a queen bed, a fireplace and dark wood furniture, with a shower bath and a separate Jacuzzi room.
  • Lilac SuiteA private sitting area on the first-floor porch, a gas fireplace and an oversized soaking tub.First-floor accessible room
  • Harvest SuiteOur newest and most expansive: two rooms, starting with a cozy living room.
  • Champagne SuiteLake and vineyard views from two large windows, a four-poster queen bed and a private door to the second-floor porch.

The rooms

Each one decorated in its own colors.

  • Lake RoomA bay window onto the vineyards and the lake, in leafy green with white accents.
  • Rose RoomDeep greens, pale rose and mahogany. Vintage-inspired and cozy.
  • Seneca RoomBold blue with pale yellow and white flowers, like bluebells, sky and water.
  • Vineyard RoomSoft earth tones, an ensuite bath and a king bed that converts into two twins.
  • Vintner’s RoomRich burgundy, gold and dark wood, like the vintner’s study at the back of the winery.
