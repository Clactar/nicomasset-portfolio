What we paint and repair

Interior, exterior and commercial work, told the way we tell it on our own page.

  • Interior painting

    Whole houses, top to bottom: walls, ceilings, trim and doors. We spray and roll, and we do offer a spray finish if you want that smooth, factory-made look.

  • Exterior painting and stain

    Siding, trim and decks. On one home we filled all the carpenter bee holes with wood dowels and epoxy, then applied two coats of solid stain. Our recommendation for decks: stain every three years.

  • Drywall repair

    Holes, water damage and opened walls. After a leak, we did the mudding, texturing and touch-up work on a ceiling we had painted before.

  • Commercial painting

    Dryfall ceiling grids, exposed basement beams and commercial deck lids. For masonry, we prime with SW Loxon and follow with Loxon XP masonry coating, so the brick can breathe.
