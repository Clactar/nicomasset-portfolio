“Little Sparrow is definitely a bright light where you can escape the stressors of daily life for a while and just create.”

Katie Wolkin, who came for a single open studio day about three years ago and has been back every week since

A calm, creative refuge from daily stress

A bright, beautiful 1,100-square-foot space designed to support artists of all skill levels. We keep the atmosphere relaxed and focused, so please set phones to silent during class.

Everything you need, already here

  • Two 23" × 27" kilns
  • Electric wheels for all skill levels
  • Griffin Grip for precise trimming
  • Slab roller and clay extruder
  • Pugmill for blending and recycling clay
  • Drying station and plaster wedging table
  • A dedicated 48" × 18" shelf for each open studio and hand-building student
  • Library of ceramics, pottery and art books
  • Fully equipped kitchen, tea and coffee
  • Private, gender-neutral restroom
  • Photography setup with professional lighting
  • Free Wi-Fi and ample free parking

The teachers

Margaret Abell

Artist & owner

An artist and graphic designer who has worked in clay on and off for 30+ years, mostly hand-building. Her work has been shown at The Little Theater Cafe Gallery, Studio 402 Gallery, Main Street Arts, Rochester Contemporary and Flower City Arts.

Zara Bronwyn Davis

Visiting artist

A ceramic and fine artist working and teaching in Rochester, with a BFA in ceramics from the Rochester Institute of Technology. She spent three years as a resident artist at Flower City Arts Center.

Christin Bentley

Visiting artist & studio tech

A ceramic educator and potter with a studio arts education from SUNY Jefferson and Potsdam. Wheel throwing and hand building are what she enjoys most.
