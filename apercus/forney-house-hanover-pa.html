A charming historic B&B in downtown Hanover

This restored 1905 house is on the National Register of Historic Places. Its suites are named after the Forney family, and the Matthias Nace Forney Room is decorated with vintage horse paintings.

Vintage horse paintings and decor

Matthias Nace Forney Room

From $160/nightQueen bed, for 2 guests

A pioneer in the early railroad world, Matthias left the area when he went off to college and spent the rest of his life in New York City. He kept a beautiful home here, at his brother's, for his visits.

This queen room has its own en-suite bathroom and is decorated with vintage horse paintings and decor.

Amanda Hinkle Forney Suite

From $175/nightKing bed, for 2 guests

This king suite is named after the matriarch of this line of the Forney family, and it is decorated where history meets modern. It has an en-suite bathroom and a separate living room with a daybed that can sleep two more guests.

David Peter Forney Suite
