The summer home of Howard Conkling

Built in 1890 for a wealthy bachelor, where no expense was spared.

A 12-foot beamed ceiling, chestnut wainscoting and keyhole staircases still frame the Great Room, now the guest sitting area. The 1890 building, with its back-to-back fireplaces, is the center for gathering; the Carriage House has its own personality.

After 40 years of the Merlinos running the Inn, George and Mary Haleem took it on as Rockwell Falls Inn and Wine Bar, preserving its charm while adding new amenities to enjoy year round. Outside, the original garden steps lead to the perennial garden and its turn-of-the-century arbors.
