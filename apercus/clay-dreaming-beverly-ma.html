Paint your own pottery

Any person of any age can paint their own unique piece. Come relax, socialize and have fun, and feel free to bring your own refreshments.

$5studio fee for the first hour, per person
$2.50per additional hour, per person

Plus the cost of the pottery you choose.

  1. Pick your pieceFrom whimsical figurines to dinnerware and housewares, with seasonal and holiday selections too.
  2. Choose your colorsOver 50 colors and types of glazes, all non-toxic and water soluble. Our staff is always there for support and creative coaching.
  3. Fire it upWe fire your pottery to a high-gloss shine, beautiful and functional, and let you know in 7–10 days when it’s ready to pick up.

Glass fusing, for grown-ups

Learn the basics of glass fusing in a fun and relaxed workshop: follow the instructor’s design, or break out with your own imagination, and create a beautiful 6 x 6 suncatcher tile. The perfect creative fix, and it’s BYOB.
