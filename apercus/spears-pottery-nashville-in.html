Spears Gallery is in a quaint, historic building in downtown Nashville, Indiana.

The gallery primarily features the finely crafted work of potter Larry Spears. It also shows the fine art photography of his son, Kyle Spears, and the sculptural ceramic works of his son Tyler Spears, along with work by numerous other craftsmen from the Brown County area and beyond: wood, jewelry, basketry, metal, paintings and more.

Larry is a primarily self-taught potter and has been working at his craft for over 30 years.

About the potter

“Focusing on the various aspects of design, my work continues to evolve around the simple correlation between form and function. Hopefully the end result is a piece that can become a part of, and enrich someone’s lifestyle.”

Larry works primarily in functional forms, in various stoneware and porcelain clay bodies, with a rich color palette and a wide variety of glazes and glazing techniques. All of the work is thrown, much of it adorned with hand-built appendages, and reduction or wood-fired. Each firing creates subtle variations.

The pottery is lead-free, dishwasher and microwave-safe, and may be used in a conventional oven.
