Learn to build your own pottery.

I offer adult classes for beginning to intermediate hand building, held at my home studio in Kyle. No clay experience is necessary. We concentrate on slab and extruding skills, and may also try pinch pots and coil building. I only have two wheels, so wheel skills come as a rotation, one project per cycle.

Hello! I’m Stacia.

My name is pronounced Stay-shuh, like station without the n, but I’ll answer to most anything! I found my love of clay in college, as an art major, when pottery was a required course. Now I can’t imagine doing anything else.

I worked at a paint-your-own-pottery shop after graduation, then in 2012 opened my own studio. I love to make functional kitchen stoneware, slip trail, and experiment with glazes. I work out of my home in Kyle, where I live with my husband and young son.

God has blessed me richly with the ability to have pottery part of my daily life, and I hope my pottery blesses you too!
