The barn we all adore

Our 60x40' post and beam barn was built in 1899 and has been standing beautifully ever since. It has held cows, classic cars, baby showers, weddings, celebrations of life and more.

In 2013, the Davis family renovated it: the walls were sandblasted and the floor engineered and secured from below to host a rockin' dance floor. The second-floor balcony, once a hay loft that could not be used, was renovated and made secure.

From dairy farm to wedding venue

The King Hill property has served many families since 1795, as a home, a dairy farm, a bed & breakfast and a wedding venue.

  1. 1795Samuel King buys the property and begins building his home.
  2. 1811His son Horatio King, later Postmaster General under President Buchanan, is born here.
  3. 1863Nearly the entire property burns to the ground.
  4. 1864The farmhouse is rebuilt; its front getting-ready space and kitchen survive from the original homestead.
  5. 1899The 60x40' post and beam barn goes up.
  6. 2013The Davis family renovates the barn, dance floor and balcony.
  7. 2015The Michaud family purchases the property.
