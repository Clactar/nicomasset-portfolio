Two brothers and a backyard campfire.

Somero Maple Farm began in 2008, when brothers Peter and Reuben, just 13 and 11, boiled their first batch of maple syrup over a campfire in the back yard.

Their passion for maple grew with them, and so did the facility, the production and the product line. Every spring, when the sap starts to flow, we work long days to make our syrup, and each batch is filtered, tasted and graded.

Winning multiple awards over the years, we are committed to the best quality maple syrup and a variety of maple products, made in the hills of southern New Hampshire.

Boiled, and boiled, and boiled.

Plain and simple, maple syrup is the sap from a maple tree boiled down to the proper density. Here is how it happens at our sugar house each spring.

40 : 1On average, it takes 40 gallons of sap to make one gallon of pure maple syrup.
  1. 1

    Tap

    When days reach the 40s and nights stay below freezing, we drill a small hole in each maple and insert a spout.

  2. 2

    Collect

    We gather some of the sap flowing up and down the tree and bring it to the sugar house.

  3. 3

    Boil

    The sap goes into the evaporator and boils down until it reaches the right density for syrup.

  4. 4

    Grade and bottle

    After filtering, every batch is tested and graded, then bottled and sealed for our friends and neighbors.

Questions we hear at the sugar house.

Pure maple syrup is simple. Here is what people ask us most.

What is 100% pure maple syrup?

It is the sap from a maple tree boiled, and boiled, and boiled down to the proper density, to create a pure, all-natural sweetener.

In the spring in New Hampshire, when temperatures are in the 40s during the day and the nights are below freezing, we tap the trees, collect the sap and boil it in the evaporator. Yes, plain and simple.

What is the difference between grades?

At the beginning of the season we generally get a lighter syrup with a delicate taste. As the season goes on, the syrup gets darker and the taste gets stronger. One grade is not “better” for you than another: it just depends on what you like.

What are the ingredients in pure maple syrup?

There is nothing added to maple syrup. It is just maple sap from the tree, boiled down into syrup.

What do I use maple sugar on?

Anything you like. It is a good substitute for brown sugar when baking, and good on toast, in your coffee or tea, on squash, and in the foil on a baked potato.

Beyond pancakes, where does the syrup go?

Besides pancakes and waffles, try our syrup in your coffee, tea or milk, hot or cold, and on your ice cream and cereal. In fact, it can replace any other sweetener.
