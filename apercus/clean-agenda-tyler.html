Our story

Clean Agenda started in 2024 with a simple belief: cleaning should be done thoughtfully.

From the beginning, we've used all-natural products made in-house, because what goes into your home matters. As the business grew, we expanded into post construction and exterior window cleaning while keeping that same commitment to quality and care.

We're not just cleaning houses. We're creating calm, clear spaces where people can actually live.
