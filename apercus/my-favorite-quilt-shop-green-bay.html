Long arm quilting services

If you would like computerized edge-to-edge quilting, or custom quilting, please contact one of these quilters.

To prepare your quilt for quilting

  1. Press all the seams of your quilt front flat and make sure the quilt lays flat. If it doesn't, there is a possibility for puckers during the quilting process.
  2. Make sure your backing is at least 4 inches larger than the quilt front on all four sides.
