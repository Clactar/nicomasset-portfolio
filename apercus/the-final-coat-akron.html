Residential painting

Interior and exterior, from small to large projects. Every project begins with a conversation about your goals, preferred colors and timeline, and a free estimate.

Interior painting process

Designed to give high quality results with minimal interruption to your home. We protect your floors, furniture and belongings first, and finish with a detailed walkthrough.

  1. Surface preparationWe repair cracks, nail pops, dents and other imperfections with minor drywall repair, sanding and patching.
  2. Surface cleaningWalls, ceilings and trim are cleaned to remove dust, oils and residue, so primers and paints bond correctly.
  3. Prime and paintPremium primers and paints selected for your surfaces: walls, ceilings, trim, doors or cabinetry.
How quickly can I get an estimate?

We do our best to work around your schedule, but typically we can get to you within 48 hours.

How long does an interior project take?

It depends on the size of the space, the number of rooms and the prep work. Typically one to two days, and we give you a proper time estimate when we come to see your space.

Do I need to move my furniture?

Move small, fragile or personal items out of the way. Our team can help move larger furniture to the center of the room and cover it with plastic or drop cloths.

Can you help me choose a color?

Absolutely. We also have a color matching service to get you the right color paint for your home.

Exterior painting

Removing old paint and flaking material, thorough cleaning, then high quality primer and paint for long-lasting protection that stands up to the elements.

We work with wood siding, aluminum, vinyl, brick, stucco, fiber cement and more, typically with PPG, Benjamin Moore and Sherwin Williams paints.
