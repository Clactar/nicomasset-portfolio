A painting company you can trust

At Four Bridges Painting, our bottom line is people: our customers come first. You have many choices for painting your home, and you want a company that delivers on its promises. With us, you get a team that does what they say they will, and leaves you thrilled with the results.

Our fun, loving and positive team takes pride in turning your vision into reality, and in keeping your project stress-free, whether we paint your exterior, your interior or your cabinets.

About Us

What we paint

Homes in and around Chattanooga, inside and out, plus the repairs that make the paint look right.

  • Exterior painting

    Walls and doors, from wood to brick and stucco, in the finish and color that suit your style. Deck staining, fascia and trim brought back to their original beauty.

  • Interior painting

    Walls and ceilings repainted for a fresh new look. Real life means handprints, pet paws and scuff marks: eventually, all walls need a fresh coat.

  • Cabinets

    We paint cabinets too, alongside home exteriors and interiors.

  • Drywall repair

    Small cracks, holes or larger issues repaired first, then a great coat of paint on top.

  • Color selection

    Choosing colors that work together is a challenge. We help you find the ones that complement your home's style.

Our painting process

From the first walk-around to the final walk-through.

  1. Estimate

    A Q&A session, a project walk-around, measurements and an on-the-spot estimate review.

  2. Color

    We help you through the abundance of color choices, so you feel confident in your selection.

  3. Prep

    A thorough clean, repair and prep of your home's surfaces for the best possible results.

  4. Paint

    Our skilled crews apply your selected paint following current industry best practices.

  5. Review

    One final quality walk-through, so every standard is met and you are 100% thrilled.

Two-Year Painting Warranty

It covers our interior and exterior paint projects. If for any reason you are not happy with your painting job, contact us within two years of the project completion and we will be happy to address the issue.

About Us: Eric Trigg, your local painter

Founder Eric Trigg has been in the painting trades for 20+ years. He learned the trade with some of Chattanooga's finest painters, spent the better part of a decade on residential and commercial properties in and around the city, then managed the paint department of a local industrial manufacturer.

Four Bridges Painting sits right in the middle, between independent contractors and large companies. We do a limited number of homes each year and keep our overhead low, so our house painting prices stay competitive.
