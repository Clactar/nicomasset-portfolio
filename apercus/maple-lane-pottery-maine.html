The designs

Every animal on the farm finds its way into the clay, in sculpture or in painting. Same animal, endless fun variations.

A small farm in Windsor

I grew up on a small farm in southern New Hampshire, and now I live and work on a small farm in Windsor, Maine, with my husband and two children.

I am very much inspired by the farm animals and wildlife around us. They keep my life busy, fun and lively, and they all end up in my clay work.

Inside the studio

“Kiln loading is a bit like a puzzle,” said my ceramics professor. “You have to optimize your space!”

When I make and glaze a piece, I think about its design and its function, and also about how it will fit in the kiln. Sometimes I wonder if I need a bigger kiln.
