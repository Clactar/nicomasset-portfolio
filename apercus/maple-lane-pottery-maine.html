Ask Robbi about mugs

“Kiln loading is a bit like a puzzle,” my ceramics professor said. “You have to optimize your space!”

When I make and glaze my pieces, I think about the design and the function, and also about how they will fit in the kiln. Sometimes I wonder if I need a bigger kiln.

The farm in the clay

I grew up on a wonderful small farm in southern New Hampshire. I now live and work on a small farm with my husband and two children in Windsor, Maine, and I am very much inspired by the farm animals and wildlife around us.

We raise chickens, cows, horses and sheep. We also have four cats, a rabbit and a dog who keep my life very busy, fun and lively.

All of these creatures find their way into my clay work, in sculpture or in painting. I love the way they take on so many different shapes, and how their different personalities make wonderful variations of the same animal.
