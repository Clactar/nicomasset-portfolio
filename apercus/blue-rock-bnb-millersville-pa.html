A colonial farmhouse, built in 1847

This Lancaster County farmhouse holds a bit of the character of each of its previous residents. On the lower level, an early 20th-century water pump fits into the antique wood paneling and solid brick walls. Upstairs, reached by a curved staircase, the original wooden floors and trim were fashioned by craftsmen of long ago.

Outdoors, enjoy a large backyard in a peaceful neighborhood, with a community park across the street and a golf course within a mile.

Within walking distance: a grocery store, several restaurants, two convenience stores, a post office, a Lancaster County RRTA bus stop and the MU campus. As far as hotels in Millersville, PA, we are it.

Cabin Family Suite

About 800 sq. ft. on one level, private entrance

Queen bed and two twin beds, sleeper sofa, futon, a kitchen with appliances and a living room with TV. Inside this daylight basement stands a 1900 water pump, among barn siding and brick walls. Suitable for the entire family.

Low-hanging ceiling beams may be a concern for guests six feet tall. Well-behaved dogs are welcome in this suite only, for a small fee.

Sun Suite

For an individual or a couple

First-floor living quarters with kitchen, dining and living room. Upstairs, a bedroom with a queen bed and a private bath (shower), opening onto the “sun” room, an enclosed porch balcony. A sleeper sofa is available for an extra person.

Dutchman Suite

Two rooms upstairs

First-floor living quarters with kitchen, dining and living room. Upstairs, the bedroom has a queen bed and a private bath (shower), with 3 twin beds in the adjoining second room.

Breakfast on the house

Start off your day of business or leisure with a complimentary home-cooked breakfast in our dining room, then set out to explore the area.

A typical morning: freshly baked muffins, homemade granola with milk, one of many home-cooked egg dishes, pancakes or French toast, fresh fruit (local in season) or homemade applesauce, sausage or bacon, pastries, hot coffee or tea, and juices.

Milk is from a local farm, and eggs come directly from the chicken house.

Meet your hosts

Innkeepers Ed and Stephanie Hersh have lived in Millersville most of their lives. Both grew up on farms in neighboring York and Dauphin counties, with PA German heritage, met at Millersville University and married in 1980.

Stephanie taught Special Education for six years in Lancaster schools, then home-educated their four children. She enjoys being a grandmother, and gardening, cooking, camping and crafts.

As a nationally certified Christian counselor, Dr. Hersh also offers optional counseling and prayer ministry for guests. For scheduling, please ask at reservation time.

Thanks to our guests, Blue Rock has been given a Best of Pennsylvania Hospitality award. We are also a TripAdvisor Recommended Hospitality site.

Finding Blue Rock

In Lancaster County’s Pennsylvania Dutch country: 3 miles from Franklin and Marshall College, within minutes of Sight and Sound Theater, and a day trip from Washington DC, Baltimore, Gettysburg, Hershey, Philadelphia and New York City.

From Baltimore or Washington DC (south)

From the Baltimore Beltway (Route 695), take Route 83 North to York, then Route 30 East to Lancaster. Exit at Rohrerstown Road (Route 741), turn right and follow 741 East 4 miles to Route 999 West / Manor Avenue. Turn right, go through two traffic lights and bear right onto Blue Rock Rd. (with 999). We are the eighth house on the left.

From Harrisburg (north)

Take the Pennsylvania Turnpike to Exit 19, then 283 East (first exit after the tollbooths) to the Lancaster area. Exit at Millersville / East Petersburg (Route 741) and follow 741 East 5 miles to Route 999 West. Turn right onto Manor Avenue, go through two traffic lights and bear right onto Blue Rock Rd. (with 999). We are the eighth house on the left.
