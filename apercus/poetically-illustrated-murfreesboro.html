Hi, I’m Destiney

My mission is to share a vibrant, fun, dynamic narrative that lets African and African American people see the beauty I see in them.

I’m a best-selling published visual artist, illustrator, and CEO of Poetically Illustrated Studios. I paint and draw people and stories that inspire me.

I discovered my artistic ability at the age of two and have called myself an artist ever since. I earned my Bachelor of Fine Arts from Mississippi State University in 2013, and with the support of my husband and my family I left my 9-to-5 to become a full-time artist and small business owner.
