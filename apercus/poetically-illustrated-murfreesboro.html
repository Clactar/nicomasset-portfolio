Destiney Powell

I am a best-selling published visual artist, an illustrator, and CEO of Poetically Illustrated Studios. My figurative and portrait-style narrative paintings share the stories of African and African American people from the past, present and future.

My mission is to share a vibrant, fun, dynamic narrative that lets African and African American people see a visual representation of the beauty I see in them.

I discovered my artistic ability at the age of two and earned my Bachelor of Fine Arts from Mississippi State University. I paint and draw people and stories that inspire me.
