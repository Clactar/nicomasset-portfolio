Commission a piece for your space.

Commissioning an artwork is all about communication. You tell me what you want and need; I listen carefully and come back with a proposal that explains how we can get there, for your space or for your organization’s mission.

  1. Design development

    Concept drawings, scale models (usually 1″ = 1′) and a written proposal with dimensions, materials, timeline and budget.

  2. Agreement

    A basic contract that restates the proposal and any additional specifications.

  3. Production

    Scheduled around the projects already in the studio when the agreement is confirmed.

  4. Site visits

    You are encouraged to visit and review the progress of the artwork.

  5. Delivery and installation

    Large pieces are relatively light: easy to move by truck, with welded steel armatures designed for the site.
