Workshops in Bell Buckle

Sign up for a workshop to refine a skill or explore your creative side. Classes are small and hands-on, held in my studio, and the pieces often end up living outdoors. Participants have come from Washington State to Maine, Florida to Minnesota. Lunches are included with all workshops.

Next year’s schedule is taking shape now. Here are the formats my students know best; some favorites will be back.

New to concrete

Garden Bowls

An introduction to concrete garden art: cast bowls in simple sand molds, for a birdbath, a butterfly bowl or a pet water bowl, then embed mosaic.

Stepping stones and beyond

Reverse Cast Mosaics

Make a mosaic, place it in a form and cast concrete over it from the back, starting from a “waste” mold that is also your template.

Foam and wet concrete

Stacked Stories

Two favorite carving materials, carved EPS foam and wet carved concrete, combined into totem elements that tell a story.

Bas relief

Off the Wall

Wall-hung pieces with sculptural qualities: layered foam, metal meshes and concrete systems, plus hanging hardware.

Some experience needed

Hybrid Armatures

Carved foam combined with rebar, wire, metal meshes and PVC to push form further than a single material allows.

Large-scale, 8 days

Build a Chair!

Everything a successful piece of public sculpture needs, learned by building your own unique chair: ergonomic design, maquettes, drawings as a construction tool, drainage and installation systems. Then there’s the mosaic.
