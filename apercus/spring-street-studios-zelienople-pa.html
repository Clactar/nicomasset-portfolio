Three ways into glass.

We specialize in fused and stained glass. Most fused classes run during drop-in hours, with no group minimums; stained glass and longer, more specialized classes are offered as open registration or by request.

Fuse it.

A good entry point into glass making: learn how to cut, decorate, melt or slump glass sheets into your own unique creation.

A studio, and a shop.

Renee Ayers and her long-time glass collaborator Elizabeth Fortunato drew up the plan for Spring Street Studios together: a teaching and working glass studio as well as a retail shop. The goal is to cultivate the existing glass community, educate and inspire those new to glass, and support our local artists.
