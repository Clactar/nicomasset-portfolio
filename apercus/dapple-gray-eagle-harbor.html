A little about us

Dapple-Gray is a full-log lodge on the south shore of the Great Inland Sea, in Michigan’s Keweenaw Peninsula. Our 27 acres along the rocky shore cliffs and crystal clear waters of Lake Superior are pristine and peaceful.

Inside, the Great Room rises around a 28-foot-high fireplace. Antique displays run through the lodge: an annotated history of American pattern glass, Victorian ceramics, tools of the mining and lumber industry, antique dolls and children’s dishes.

Your ears may delight to the sounds of the Lake, whether a wild “blow” or a gentle breeze. A great escape from your everyday life.

The antique shop, inside the lodge

General line Victorian, with an emphasis on glass and ceramics. Everything but the display cases is for sale.

Meet our namesake

The Dapple-Gray is a hand-carved steeplechase horse built for patrons of the 1915 Pan-Pacific Exposition in San Francisco. He has welcomed visitors to Ruth’s shop for 35 years, and he will greet you in the entry.
