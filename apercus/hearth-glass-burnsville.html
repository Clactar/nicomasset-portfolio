Our history

Hearth Glass and Gallery was established in 2022 by Keikichi Littleton. It continues a proud legacy in glassworking that traces back to her grandfather, Harvey K. Littleton, a renowned figure in the Studio Glass Movement in the United States.

Here in the Toe River Valley it is a vibrant hub for both budding and experienced glass artists. By offering education and resources, Hearth fosters an environment where glass art is cultivated, celebrated and marketed, so the craft of glassblowing is passed down through generations.

FAQs

Can we come in to see glass blowing?

Yes. Hearth is an open-access studio with daily glass blowing demonstrations and classes. The public is invited to watch the artists at work, 10am to 5pm, Tuesday through Saturday.

Are you kid and pet friendly?

Yes, but children must be accompanied by a credit card at all times. All pieces are fragile, so no running or cartwheels in the gallery. Pets are welcome too, as long as they are leashed and well behaved.

Do you offer custom orders?

Yes, Hearth and many of the artists who rent at our studio do custom work. Please allow up to 8 weeks for your custom order to be ready.

Do you repair broken art glass?

Not at this time, but we are happy to help you create a one of a kind replacement.
