A friend, $100 and two pickup truckloads of firewood.

Ever since 1980, when I traded a friend $100 and two pickup truckloads of firewood for an old Minolta camera and two lenses, my life has not been the same.

On these pages, you'll experience some of the most beautiful places in America. Some images are snapshots, but most are my attempt to present new and unique vignettes of those places.

“Sometimes I succeed photographically, but many times I just enjoy the walk in the woods, the mountains, beside streams and waterfalls.”

Red River Gorge, My Second Home

A hardbound coffee-table book, with Acclaim Press

The dramatic landscape, vibrant scenery and rich foliage of eastern Kentucky's Red River Gorge, near Natural Bridge in the Daniel Boone National Forest, with first-hand accounts of my experiences there, a section on great photo spots within “the gorge” and tips for making better photographs. The foreword is by Oscar Geralds, Jr., who helped lead the fight in the late 1960's to prevent a lake that would have flooded the gorge.
