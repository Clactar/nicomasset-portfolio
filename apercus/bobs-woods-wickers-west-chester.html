Baskets, turned lids, finials and furniture, all from my shop.

Experience and creativity

Nantucket baskets came first. I taught myself to weave them on the mold, and I have completed more than 300 since 2005.

When I retired in 2016 I started woodworking, and now the two crafts meet: woven bodies, turned lids, bands and finials. I enjoy the variety of woodturning, woodworking and basket weaving.

  • Master Artisan, Pennsylvania Guild of Craftsmen
  • Member, Keystone Woodturners

Workshops I teach

I teach hands-on classes through the Pennsylvania Guild of Craftsmen. Pick the one that tempts you and ask me when it runs next.

Nantucket basket

You will learn the process of weaving a Nantucket basket on a mold. Your basket will be between 4 and 6 inches in diameter.

Turn a pen

Getting started in woodturning? It is easy to learn and fun to do. You will learn the ABCs of turning, the types of turning, which tools to use for what, sharpening and safety, and you will turn a spindle and a small bowl.

Bring a face shield and a dust mask. No loose clothing.

Pysanky egg

A two-hour beginner class in the art of pysanky, Ukrainian Easter eggs, decorated with dyes, beeswax and a stylus. All materials are included.
