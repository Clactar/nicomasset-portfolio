Its graceful early 20th-century architecture, second story balcony, and rocking chairs on the front porch invite guests to enjoy a morning cup of coffee or glass of wine outdoors.

Beyond the entry parlor is the sun-filled dining room with its large bay window and elegant antique back bar. Upstairs, all 10 guest rooms feature private baths, comfortable bedding, unique furnishings, and wireless 5G internet.

An outdoor hot tub invites you to soak under the Valley’s legendary night skies. And the Bross is just one block from Grand Avenue, so you can walk to entertainment venues, shopping, theater, and restaurants.

Your innkeepers, Suzanne & Mike

Innkeepers Suzanne Tripp and Mike Yengling bring the perfect skillset to the hotel. Suzanne is a historical architect by training, with a background in baking and a lifelong love of cooking.

Mike has a background in historic preservation, a jack-of-all-trades mentality, and loves to pass on local knowledge, be it a hike, bike ride, scenic attraction, or offbeat culinary find.

Enjoy the abundance of the North Fork Valley, delicious gourmet breakfasts, and their hospitality. We look forward to your stay!

Delta County’s oldest hotel

  1. 1903

    William Taylor Bross buys the two vacant lots next door and builds the hotel.

  2. 1906

    The Bross Hotel opens for business.

  3. 1906 to 1921

    W.T. Bross, deputy sheriff for this part of the county, meets the train each afternoon and brings guests in his wagon, while Mrs. Bross serves as hostess and manager. The hotel is both a lodging place and the finest restaurant in town.

  4. 2020

    Suzanne Tripp and Mike Yengling move to Paonia and take over the hotel that September, adding thoughtful aesthetic touches, room upgrades, an ever-evolving breakfast menu, wine service and live music.

Discover the Paonia charm
