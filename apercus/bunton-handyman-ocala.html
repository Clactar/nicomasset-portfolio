My services

Reliable handyman services for everyday home needs: the jobs people don't have the time, tools, or ability to handle themselves.

What I don't take on

Large projects or HVAC jobs. My focus is strictly on handyman services done right.

  • Minor home repairs

    Fixing doors, repairing fixtures, tightening loose fittings, patching damage and general wear and tear around the house.

  • Furniture assembly

    Flat-pack furniture, bedroom sets, office desks, chairs and shelves, assembled correctly, safely and securely.

  • Flooring repairs and installations

    Damaged sections, loose boards, areas that need replacement, and full installations for rooms being renovated.

  • Basic plumbing tasks

    Minor leaks, repairing or replacing faucets, tightening fittings and simple adjustments that don't need major system work.

  • Siding installation and repairs

    Vinyl, fiber cement, wood and composite siding, installed or repaired: cracks, warping, moisture damage and general wear, for your home or business.
