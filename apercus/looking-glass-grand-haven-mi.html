Your home away from home, on top of a Michigan dune

A peaceful place to get away for a weekend or the week. Every stay includes beach towels, chairs and umbrellas, so there is no extra gear to pack, and you can rent bikes right at the inn to explore the waterfront and the trails nearby.

Every detail is meant to feel like home, with products from Michigan companies: Lady Americana mattresses and Down Inc. pillows from Grand Rapids, goat milk soap by Milk & Quackers from Battle Creek.

Downtown Grand Haven is one mile down the shore

Walk or drive into a charming beach town, then come back to watch the sunset over Lake Michigan from the deck. We are 35 minutes or less from Grand Rapids, Muskegon, GVSU and Holland.

“The small town that America is fighting to keep.”
How one visitor described Grand Haven

In town

Three coffee shops, four breweries, three distilleries, a wine tasting room, more than a dozen restaurants and just as many dessert spots.

Out and about

A local museum, art galleries, live music, the musical fountain and festivals all year round.

Outdoors

The beach across the street, and hiking and biking trails a short drive away. Bikes can be rented at the inn.
