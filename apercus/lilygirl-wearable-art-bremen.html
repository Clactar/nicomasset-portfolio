It all starts with the stone.

For me, a self-taught artist, the gemstones and pearls I like to work with have something special about them: an unusual shape, exceptional color, or a rough gem that just needs a little care and attention.

I use traditional lapidary practices to bring these stones and fossils from rough to jewelry ready. Most of my gemstones and fossils come directly from the people who mine them, and my South Sea and Tahitian pearls come directly from the pearl farm owners.

Natural stones onlyNo glassNo acrylicNo man-made stones

Fine silver and 18K gold, hammered cold.

Their color and patina complement each other and the gemstones they adorn. I love the way they can be hammered, formed and manipulated into shape with cold processes and cold connection techniques.

  • SheetI start with a sheet of fine silver or 18K gold and cut out a shape to work with.
  • FormI hammer and form it into a cap, setting or decoration.
  • WireFine silver, sterling and 18K gold wire become flourishes, swirls, boules and bails.

Buying jewelry art.

Availability
There is only one of each piece. Email or call to see if the jewelry you want is still available. Some items are on my Etsy shop.
Special orders
Available upon request. I may be able to make something similar to a piece that is no longer available; the sterling and gemstones will be different.
Sizing
Pendants are width first, then height, without the bail. Rings can be reduced a couple of sizes or made one size larger.
Shipping
All orders are insured and shipped within 48 hours of payment confirmation.
Repairs and returns
If you have any problems with my jewelry, I will be more than glad to repair it. Not pleased? Call me for a return authorization.

All jewelry art comes with a signed Lilygirl tag listing all the materials used, beautifully wrapped and ready for gift giving.
