“He told me I must learn to appreciate shades of green and texture.”The master gardener, at a shade garden seminar

Color is an essential in my life. Rich color!

Many years ago, I attended a shade garden seminar to gain insight into colorful plants I could nurture and grow in my wooded yard. It was there that my perception of all I see around me was forever changed by a few simple words said by the master gardener.

Because of those simple words, the sight through my lens now appears with more depth and definition of texture. I use these visual images to design depth into an otherwise flat piece of glass.

Color is empowering for me and enriches my life. I want women to recognize and celebrate their inner and outer beauty.

Glass, cord & copper

Depth, out of an otherwise flat piece of glass.

By layering opaque, transparent, powder and frit glasses, in a sequence of firings and cold-working, I am able to obtain texture and depth in my work.

  1. LayeringOpaque, transparent, powder and frit glasses.
  2. FiringsNot one, but a sequence of firings.
  3. Cold-workingFor texture and depth in the finished piece.
A bold wearable art, with an empowering pop of color that never oversteps your comfort level.

I know many women like color but are intimidated by it. My goal in creating Elucido Glass jewelry is to offer women a bold wearable art that gives them their empowering pop of color without overstepping their comfort level.
