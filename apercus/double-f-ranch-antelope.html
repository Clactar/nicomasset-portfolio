How do I know? Because I've been there.

When I first started my business I sold whole and half beef.

The process was confusing for customers: they had no idea what they were ordering, how much space they needed, and what it was going to actually cost. Six months later, I heard the same feedback over and over. The beef was fantastic, but:

  • All the "good" cuts had been eaten already
  • They developed "freezer fatigue" and were so tired of eating beef
  • They felt so guilty buying other meats with beef already in the freezer

So I went to work solving each one of these challenges, and came up with the simplest, most streamlined way to get delicious, pastured meats from my farm to your freezer.

Because figuring this stuff out is my job. Not yours.

Conscious Quantities

A whole year's worth of meat is overwhelming, and so is grocery shopping every single week. Stocking up every few months is the sweet spot. You don't have to be a big family or buy a whole cow: we offer 25# and 50# bundles.

Conscientious Eaters

When you ordered the right quantity for your household, there's no pressure to "hurry and use it up." For a couple, a small 50# share equates to eating meat about once per week over a year.

What's in my share?

"A mix of steaks, roasts, and ground meats" isn't good enough. I'll give you a specific pack list so you know exactly what you are ordering before clicking "Buy".

No ongoing commitment

I'll keep you posted on upcoming offerings, and you sign up for ONLY the deliveries that work for you. Fill your freezer on YOUR timeline.

"If you are what you eat, then what did your meat eat?"

Situated in the high desert of Central Oregon, Double F Ranch has been farmed by the Forman family for over a hundred years. Homesteaded in 1902 by Frank M. Forman, it is managed today by the 4th and 5th generations of the family.

Originally a dryland wheat farm, our diversified ranch raises small grains and specialty crops and runs a herd of beef cattle. By growing, grinding and mixing our own custom ration, "quality in" equals "quality out": richly flavored, nutrient-dense meat that tastes like real food.

Hey! We're Nathan and Katia, the public faces of Double F Ranch. Community support is the foundation of our operation, and we could not do it without our customers, our friends and our family.

Get the skinny

The questions everyone asks before their first Stock Up Day.

What cuts do I get?

Good cuts only: the "greatest hits" of beef or pork. Choose beef only, beef and pork mix, pork only or ground meats only, and you get a specific pack list before you buy. The pork share includes sausage and bacon. Steaks and chops contain 2 portions per package.

How much meat will I receive?

25# or 50#, and you can order multiple share types. 25# comes packed in one very full canvas grocery bag.

How much does it cost?

25# of ground meats is $300, 25# of mixed cuts is $400, 50# of mixed cuts is $750. Same price for cash, check or card, with no fees.

When do I get it?

On your chosen Stock Up Day: Bend on Friday, November 6 from 3 to 5pm, or Portland on Sunday, November 8 from 12 to 2pm. Next come March, July and November 2027, dates TBD.

How much freezer space do I need?

Each 25# bag is slightly less than 1 cubic foot of space. You don't have to be a master at "Freezer Tetris" to use this number as a good guide.
