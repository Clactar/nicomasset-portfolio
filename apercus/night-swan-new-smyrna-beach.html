It isn't a B&B without a good breakfast

Served in the Main House from 8:00 to 9:30 am, every day. A small buffet of fresh fruit, Greek yogurt and homemade granola, fresh-squeezed orange and grapefruit juice in season, and one hot entrée a day.

  • Glorious Morning muffinsApples, carrots, coconut, pecans and dried cranberries.
  • From the ovenGinger scones, cinnamon rolls, lemon pound cake, cranberry orange muffins.
  • The hot entréePotato hash with scrambled eggs, roasted tomato pesto Parmesan quiche, breakfast sandwiches, waffles, French toast.

Gluten-free, dairy-free, vegetarian or vegan? Tell us when you book and we'll plan for it. And we can always whip up some eggs.

Three houses, sixteen rooms

The Main House, the Cygnet House and the Guest Cottage each have their own feel, and guests always seem to find a room that is just right. Both houses have guest kitchens with a refrigerator and microwave, wine glasses and silverware, so you can bring dinner back to your room.

On the water, in an Old Florida town

Our guests have use of five boat slips at the deep water dock, right across the street from the Main House. Being this close to the shore, there are plenty of ways to get out on the water.

Weddings under the live oaks

The view of the water and the shade of our live oak canopy make for a ceremony, a reception, or both, with up to 100 guests. We'll recommend a local photographer, florist, tent rental and limousine service.

There are sleeping room requirements for using our facilities: contact us for details specific to your wedding.
